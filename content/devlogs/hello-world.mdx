This is my first devlog entry — a more casual, journal-style companion to the main blog. While the blog focuses on longer-form technical writing and tutorials, this space is where I'll share:

- **What I'm building** right now
- **Problems I'm debugging** and how I solved them
- **Quick thoughts** on tools, libraries, and workflows
- **Behind-the-scenes** of projects before they're polished enough for a full blog post

## Why a Devlog?

I wanted a space that feels less formal than the blog. Something closer to a developer's notebook — quick, honest, and updated as I go. The blog is for finished thoughts; the devlog is for thinking out loud.
